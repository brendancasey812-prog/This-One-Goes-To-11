The why

Ten is fine. Eleven is the story you tell later.

Every trip has a version that works — the one the guidebook hands you, where the timing is sensible and nothing goes sideways. That's a ten. It's fine.

Eleven is the wrong turn that ended at the better beach. The dinner you got talked into. The train you took because it was slower. This blog exists because those moments never fit in a text message home, and I got tired of losing them.

So: two tabs, no fuss. Trips is the long version, one place at a time. Recommendations is the short version — the things I'd tell you over a drink if you said you were going.

About me

Hi — I'm the one holding the camera.

I plan about sixty percent of a trip and leave the rest to whoever I meet on day two. I will always pick the coastal road, the market breakfast, and the place with a handwritten menu. I am reliably wrong about how long a hike takes.

I started keeping notes because friends kept asking the same question — where should we go? — and I kept giving worse answers than the trip deserved. This is the better answer.
