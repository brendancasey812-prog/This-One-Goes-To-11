Who am I?

I'm a semi-retired Alzheimer's and brain health researcher who lives in the San Francisco Bay Area with my husband, Brian, and semi-famous Cat from Hell, Autumn. We have two grown kids who sometimes still like to travel with us.

I'm at a place in my life where I'm ready to explore the world, and I do a LOT of research before going on trips.

I'm not in this to make money, just to share knowledge.

What Will You Learn From Me?

My favorite travel experiences include a combo of natural beauty, urban culture, and great food. My favorite things are usually outdoors — hiking, biking, lounging — but I also enjoy a good museum. Either way, I want to reward myself with a fabulous meal.

So if that's what you like, keep reading!

Why "This Trip Goes to 11"?

For people of a certain age, the 1984 mockumentary This is Spinal Tap by Rob Reiner is part of our zeitgeist. In one of the most iconic scenes, guitarist Nigel Tufnel is showing off his custom guitar amplifier, pointing out that its volume knobs are "one louder" than standard amps because they go up to 11 instead of 10.

When asked by the interviewer why he doesn't simply make the volume of 10 louder, Tufnel replies, "But this one goes to 11."

For me, "taking something to 11" means doing everything in my power to make an experience as amazing as possible.
